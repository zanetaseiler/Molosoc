Cracked heels

Cracked heels: what's really going on, and what helps

Cracked heels happen when skin loses the elasticity to stretch under pressure — not from bad luck, and not from "just dry skin." The fix is straightforward once it's consistent, not occasional.

The causes

What causes cracked heels

Dry skin losing its ability to stretch

Heel skin cracks when it dries out and loses the elasticity it needs to stretch under pressure.

Pressure and weight-bearing on the heel edge

The heel's outer edge carries the most weight with every step — dry, inflexible skin can't take that pressure without splitting.

Standing all day, especially in open-back shoes

Standing for long stretches in open-back shoes speeds up both problems at once: more pressure, more moisture loss.

When it gets serious

When cracked heels go from dry to painful

Painful deep cracked heels

A crack that hurts with every step has gone past surface dryness into a deeper layer of skin.

Severe cracked heels

Severe cracked heels widen, deepen, and can bleed — an occasional cream application isn't enough at this stage.

Why "just moisturizing more" stops working at this stage

Cream that isn't sealed against skin rubs off before it's absorbed — more product doesn't fix that, more contact time does.
