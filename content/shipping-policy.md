# Shipping Policy

*Last updated: 31.7.2026*

This Shipping Policy explains how MOLOSOC processes and delivers orders placed through www.molosoc.com.

## Order Processing

Orders are typically processed and shipped within 24 hours of payment confirmation. You will receive an order confirmation email once your payment has been successfully received, and a separate notification once your order has shipped.

## Carriers

Orders are shipped via PPL, including delivery to PPL parcel shops/pickup points where selected at checkout. Delivery times are estimates provided by the carrier and may vary due to circumstances outside our control, including carrier delays, customs processing, weather, or public holidays.

## Delivery Address

Orders are delivered to the address provided by the customer at checkout. Please make sure your shipping details are accurate and complete — we are not responsible for delays or non-delivery caused by incorrect address information provided at the time of order.

## Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your delivery method and location, and are shown before you complete your order.

## Delays

While we make every effort to ship orders promptly, MOLOSOC is not responsible for delays caused by the carrier, customs authorities, or other circumstances beyond our reasonable control.
