## H1: How to fix cracked heels permanently

Cracked heels come back because treating one episode doesn't change what caused it — permanence comes from consistency, not a stronger product.

---

## H2: Why cracked heels come back after treatment

### H3: Treating the crack without treating the cause
Healing the crack itself resolves what's visible; it doesn't change the dryness and pressure that caused it, so the same spot is still vulnerable afterward.

### H3: Why the same pressure points keep failing the same way
The heel's outer edge takes the same weight-bearing pressure with every step, regardless of how well the last crack healed — so without ongoing care, it's set up to fail the same way again.

### H3: What "permanently" actually requires — consistency, not a stronger product
Lasting improvement comes from an ongoing routine, not a single more powerful treatment — the skin needs regular care indefinitely, not just until the current crack closes.

---

## H2: Severe cracked heels

### H3: When cracks have become a repeating cycle, not a one-off
When the same crack reopens repeatedly, or new ones appear in the same spot every few months, it's no longer an isolated incident — it's a cycle that needs breaking, not another one-time fix.

### H3: Why severity increases if the same pattern isn't broken
Each time a crack reopens without the underlying cause addressed, the skin around it becomes more compromised, which tends to make each episode a little worse than the last.

### H3: What changes once the routine actually holds
Once care becomes consistent rather than reactive, the cycle of reopening slows — skin gets a real chance to stay ahead of the pressure instead of catching up after each crack.

---

## H2: What makes a fix stick, not just work once

### H3: Why one good week doesn't mean it's fixed
A week of visible improvement is a sign the routine is working, not a sign it's safe to stop — the underlying dryness and pressure haven't gone anywhere.

### H3: Locking in moisture consistently instead of only when it's bad
Sealing moisture in as a regular habit, not just during a flare-up, is what actually keeps skin ahead of the pressure instead of reacting after cracks reappear.

### H3: Turning it into a five-minute habit, not a project you restart
The ongoing effort is small — a few minutes applying cream and sealing it in — but it only works as a standing habit, not something picked back up each time a crack returns.
