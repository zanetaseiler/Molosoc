## H1: Cracked heels cream: what actually makes one work

A cracked-heel cream doesn't fail because of the formula nearly as often as it fails because of the routine around it — ingredients matter less than whether the cream gets a real chance to absorb.

---

## H2: What to actually look for in a cracked heels cream

### H3: Urea and other humectants — what they actually do
Urea and similar humectants draw moisture into skin and help soften thickened areas, which is why they show up often in creams aimed at cracked heels specifically.

### H3: Thicker balms vs. lighter lotions — when each makes sense
Thicker balms sit on skin longer and suit more severe cracking, while lighter lotions absorb faster and work well for everyday maintenance once the worst of it has healed.

### H3: Why ingredients matter less than how consistently it's used
A well-formulated cream applied inconsistently underperforms a simpler one used every day with enough contact time to actually absorb.

---

## H2: Best cream for cracked heels

### H3: What "best" really depends on — severity, not brand
The "best" cream depends far more on how severe the cracking is than on which brand made it — a mild case and a deep, painful crack call for different levels of intervention, not different logos.

### H3: Why the most expensive option isn't always the most effective
Price doesn't reliably predict how well a cream performs on cracked heels — plenty of inexpensive formulas contain the same core humectants as premium ones.

### H3: The cream you already own is probably fine
If there's already a foot cream in the drawer, it's very likely capable of doing the job — the missing piece is usually consistency and contact time, not a better product.

---

## H2: Why a good cream still doesn't fix cracked heels

### H3: The routine most people already try
Most people already own a cream and already apply it somewhat regularly — the routine itself isn't the gap.

### H3: Where that routine breaks down
Cream applied and left uncovered rubs off on socks or floors within minutes, long before it's had time to actually absorb into cracked, thickened skin.

### H3: What actually needs to happen for cream to work
Cream needs sustained, sealed contact with skin to work — not a stronger formula, just enough uninterrupted time for it to actually absorb.
