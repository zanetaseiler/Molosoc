## H1: Dry skin on feet: why it keeps coming back

Feet dry out differently than the rest of the body, and moisturizing alone often doesn't fix it — not because the cream fails, but because it rarely stays on long enough to actually work.

---

## H2: What causes dry skin on feet

### H3: Why soles dry out differently than the rest of the body
Soles have no oil glands the way most of the rest of the skin does, so they rely entirely on external moisture to stay soft — which is why they dry out faster and more often.

### H3: Socks, shoes, and trapped moisture loss
Enclosed feet lose moisture to fabric and air circulation throughout the day, often without anyone noticing until the skin already feels rough.

### H3: Seasonal triggers (heating, cold air, hot showers)
Indoor heating, cold outside air, and hot showers all strip moisture from skin faster than usual, which is why dryness tends to spike in the same seasons every year.

---

## H2: Why are my feet so dry even when I moisturize?

### H3: The routine most people already try
Most people already apply cream regularly and still end up with dry feet — the routine itself isn't missing.

### H3: Why cream doesn't stay on long enough to actually absorb
Cream applied and left uncovered gets absorbed by socks or rubbed off on floors within minutes, long before the skin has had a real chance to take it in.

### H3: What actually needs to change for it to stick
Cream needs uninterrupted contact with skin, not a thicker layer or a pricier formula, to actually absorb instead of transferring somewhere else.

---

## H2: Socks, tights, and the dryness nobody talks about

### H3: Why enclosed feet dry out differently
Feet inside socks and tights all day are constantly losing moisture to fabric, which is a slower, quieter version of the same drying process as bare, exposed skin.

### H3: Winter habit-building vs. summer exposure
Winter hides feet from view, which makes it a low-pressure window to build a consistent moisture habit — without the urgency summer's visibility usually creates.

### H3: A private, low-effort ritual for this window
A short, private session with a cream already on hand fits easily into a winter routine, with none of the pressure of getting "sandal-ready."
