# Refund & Return Policy

*Last updated: 31.7.2026*

This Refund & Return Policy explains your right to return products purchased from MOLOSOC and how refunds are handled.

## Your Right to Return (EU Consumers)

In accordance with EU consumer protection law, you have the right to withdraw from your purchase within **14 days** of receiving your order, without giving any reason.

### Conditions for Return

- The product must be unused, unopened, and in its original packaging.
- For hygiene reasons, products cannot be returned once their protective seal or packaging has been opened.

### How to Start a Return

To exercise your right of withdrawal, contact us at **hello@molosoc.com** with your order number before sending anything back. We'll confirm the return address and next steps.

Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility, unless the return is due to a defective or incorrect item.

## Refunds

Once we receive your returned item and confirm it meets the conditions above, your refund will be processed within **14 days**, using the same payment method you used for the original purchase.

Original shipping costs are non-refundable, except where required by law.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If your order arrives damaged or defective, contact us as soon as possible at **hello@molosoc.com** with:

- Your order number
- A description of the issue
- Photos showing the damage or defect

We'll resolve this in accordance with applicable Czech consumer protection law — typically with a replacement or a full refund, including return shipping in these cases.

## Questions

If you have any questions about a return or refund, reach out to:

**hello@molosoc.com**
